No accounts to create. No ads. No trackers. Just your money, on your device, synced through your own iCloud.

Privacy by design

Your finances are nobody else’s business.

SpendCue is built so the developer never collects, sells, rents, or tracks your personal finance records.

No account needed

No developer sign-up and no bank connection credentials.

No ads or trackers

No advertising SDKs and no cross-app advertising tracking.

On-device intelligence

Insights use Apple’s on-device language model where available.

Apple handles payments

Billing, renewals, and refunds run through the App Store.

Where your data goes

Your records make one trip: between your devices.

Finance records live on your iPhone and sync only through your private iCloud account. They are not sent to developer servers, ad networks, or analytics backends.
